How it works

From first message to test day, in four steps.

  1. Get in touch

    Message us with your area and the service you need and we’ll provide a quote.

  2. Secure your booking

    Confirm your booking with us and we’ll get everything arranged.

  3. Learn to drive

    We confirm your lessons and arrange pick-up and drop-off where possible.

  4. Take your test

    Once your test date is confirmed, you pay the outstanding balance and test at your DLTC.
