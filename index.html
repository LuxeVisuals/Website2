How it works

From first message to test day, in four steps.

  1. Get in touch

    Message us with your area and the package you want.

  2. Pay your deposit

    Your deposit secures your booking.

  3. Learn to drive

    We confirm your lessons and arrange pick-up and drop-off where possible.

  4. Take your test

    Once your test date is confirmed, you pay the balance and test at your DLTC.
