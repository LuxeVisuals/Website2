Common Questions

How do I book?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or use the form on our contact page. Tell us your area and the service you need and we’ll provide a free quote and confirm the details.

When do I pay the balance?

Your outstanding balance is due once your test date has been confirmed.

What department fees do I need to pay?

All DLTC and traffic department fees are payable by the applicant and are not included in our package prices. We will advise you of the applicable fees when we confirm your booking.

How many lessons are included in the licence and combo packages?

The Driving Licence Only package and the Learner’s & Licence Package both include 8–10 lessons covering parking manoeuvres and road driving. Any additional lessons required will be added to your outstanding balance.

Do you pick me up for lessons?

Yes, we offer student pick-up and drop-off depending on your location. Tell us where you live and we’ll confirm.

Where do I take my test?

We work with the Brakpan, Springs and Boksburg DLTC offices.

Which areas do you cover?

Benoni, Boksburg, Springs, Brakpan and surrounding areas. If you’re unsure, get in touch and ask.

Do you offer after-hours lessons?

Yes, times after hours can be arranged. Contact us to discuss availability.
