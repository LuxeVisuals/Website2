Common questions

How do I book?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or use the form on our contact page. Tell us your area and the package you want, then pay the deposit to secure your booking.

When do I pay the balance?

You pay the outstanding balance once your test date has been confirmed.

Do you pick me up for lessons?

Yes, we offer student pick-up and drop-off depending on your location. Tell us where you live and we’ll confirm.

Where do I take my test?

We work with the Brakpan, Springs and Boksburg DLTC offices.

Which areas do you cover?

Benoni, Boksburg, Springs, Brakpan and surrounding areas. If you’re unsure, get in touch and ask.

Which licence code are these prices for?

All prices on this page are for Code 8.

Tell us where you live and which package you want. We’ll confirm the details and your pick-up arrangements.
